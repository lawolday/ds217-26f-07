This line chart shows the percent of patients meeting their weekly exercise goal in the Home-based and Center-based programs. The x-axis shows follow-up visits 1 to 4 and the y-axis shows the percent of patients meeting the goal. Home-based increased from 58% at visit 1 to 70% at visit 4. Center-based increased from 57% to 79%. At visit 4 the Center-based program was 9 percentage points higher than Home-based. Patients chose their own program so this does not prove that one program caused better results. Each percentage only includes patients seen at that visit with about 45 patients per program. The difference could also be due to chance or which patients returned.
